Scilophone is a science media project. On [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/scilophone/) we pick out the single most important idea in a topic and fit it into a few slides.

A few slides can't hold everything. The context, the evidence, the caveats and the sources get cut. This site is where they go. Every article here is the full version of one of our posts, written to be read from start to finish.

## How the articles work

- **Start with the post.** Each article opens with a short summary and a link to the Instagram post it expands.
- **Then the full story.** The article explains the idea properly, with figures and equations where they help.
- **Sources, always.** Claims are backed by numbered references at the end of every article.
- **Two languages.** Articles are published in English and Traditional Chinese. Use the language link at the top of any page to switch.
